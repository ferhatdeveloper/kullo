# Brag Plan: Seyyare

## What is this app?
Seyyare (seyyare.men) is Iraq’s AI-backed vehicle marketplace — buy, rent, auction, and ride in one orange-flame branded app across Erbil, Baghdad, and beyond.

## The angle
One country, one flame mark, every car path: search → list → ride. Not “another classifieds site” — the Iraq car OS with AI assistant, live auctions, and Hatwan wallet.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-bleed night road + flame mark slam: giant italic “pazarı” under “Irak araç”. Orange `#FF6A00` pulse. First line viewers read: **Irak araç pazarı**.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Floating search bar in use: type “Land Cruiser”, pick Erbil, hit orange “ilan ara”
- Service strip reveals: Taksi · Kiralama · Müzayede · Cüzdan
- Live auction card: Toyota Land Cruiser 2021 — 95.000.000 IQD, Erbil

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup + domain: **seyyare.men** — “Hayalindeki aracı bul.”

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry — hero search (“Hayalindeki aracı bul”)
2. Key action — filter city + budget chip, submit search
3. Result — featured listing / live auction bid card

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium Middle East marketplace launch film with flame accent
- Interpretation: Confident holds, clean type, orange as the only loud color; motion is smooth not chaotic.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0B0B0C` (ink) with paper panels `#F4F4F6`
- Accent: `#FF6A00` (flame) / deep `#E24A00`
- Text: `#FFFFFF` on ink, `#111111` on paper
- Display font: DM Sans (project) — Bold / Medium
- Body font: DM Sans
- Strongest visual element: seyyare flame mark + hero “Irak araç *pazarı*” + orange search CTA

## Share copy (draft)
Irak’ta araç al, kirala, müzayede et, yolculuk et — hepsi seyyare.men’de. 🚗🔥

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with polished UI accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (upbeat marketplace energy; strong cues at 1.60, 5.80, 8.96, 12.65, 17.91)
- Music treatment: start ~0s at volume 0.45, gentle fade-in 0.4s; fade under final logo from ~18.5s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`; lock hook to ~1.60s, services reveal ~5.80s, auction ~8.96s, outro ~17.91s
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; flame mark glow breathes with RMS — no waveform
- SFX posture: sparse; click on search submit, soft drop on cards, bong on logo
- Audio-coupled moments: typed search text, service chips arrive in sequence, auction bid counter punch
- Restraint rule: no chaotic whooshes; never mask the brand orange with loud SFX piles

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook / Hero — 3.5s
Ink ground, hero car photo veiled dark, flame mark top-left, giant headline “Irak araç” + italic orange “pazarı”, sub “Hayalindeki aracı bul — al, kirala, yolculuk et”.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: confident open; music bed lands
Audio-coupled idea: title settle near strong cue 1.60s
Music: warm bed in
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Search in use — 4.5s
Paper search card floats over hero: placeholder types “Land Cruiser”, city select Erbil, orange CTA “ilan ara” taps.
Sequential/interaction: yes — characters type, then city, then CTA pulse
Audio intent: UI clicks + key feel
Audio-coupled idea: typing + click on submit (~5.8s)
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Services strip — 4.0s
Four service tiles on paper: Taksi / Kiralama / Müzayede / Cüzdan with short desc from product copy.
Sequential/interaction: yes — tiles stagger in
Audio intent: soft drops per card
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card (~8.9s peak)
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Live auction — 4.0s
Auction card: Land Cruiser photo, “Canlı müzayede”, bid 95.000.000 IQD, city Erbil, flame badge.
Sequential/interaction: bid amount counts up then holds
Audio intent: punchy settle
Audio-coupled idea: counter + impact accent
Transition mood: dramatic soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 4.0s
Centered flame mark + “seyyare.men” + line “Hayalindeki aracı bul.” Orange underglow.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: music fades; logo bong rings
Audio-coupled idea: final logo at ~17.9s
Transition mood: hold to black
